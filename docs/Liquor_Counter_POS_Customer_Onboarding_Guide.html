1 Executive Overview & Platform Capabilities

Liquor Counter POS solves the high-volume, compliance-intensive challenges unique to Indian liquor outlets: strict state excise regulations, tax-inclusive MRP pricing, physical bottle breakage, dual counter sales (Walk-in vs Janata rates), customer khata credit, and multi-outlet syndicate cash control.

What the System Delivers

⚡ High-Speed Scanner POS

Rapid barcode scan & peg billing. Keyboard shortcuts, offline synchronization for network cuts, exact discount authorization guards, and customer returns with automatic stock replenishment.

📋 Daily Counter Sheets

The gold standard of liquor retail verification: Sold = Opening + Inward + Transfer − Closing. Physical counts reconcile against POS bills to detect leakage or unbilled sales instantly.

🚚 Smart Excise Inward Parser

Upload state excise PDF challans (e.g. Telangana ICDC). Automatically extracts line items, batch numbers, case counts, and line cost without manual entry errors.

⚖️ Dual Counters (Walk-in / Janata)

Support separate physical counters and price lists for premium Walk-in vs Janata buyers. Inter-counter bottle transfers maintain exact sub-inventory counts.

🍽️ Bar & Table Service (KOT)

Table session management with separate Kitchen and Bar Order Tickets (KOT), peg/serving measures, and instant bill generation at table checkout.

📖 Customer Khata (Credit Ledger)

Manage customer credit accounts with strict credit limits, repayment receipts via UPI/Cash/Card, and real-time aging buckets (0-15, 16-30, 31-60, 60+ days).

🔒 Cash Drawer & Shift Control

Cashiers open shifts with an authorized cash float, perform mid-day cash drops, and close shifts with exact denomination counts to prevent drawer shortages.

🏦 Bank Statement Reconciliation

Universal parser for all Indian banks. Verifies running balance chains, flags gateway charge deductions, and auto-matches batch card/UPI settlements.

Core Architecture Invariants

Domain Rules That Protect Your Profits
  • Non-Overridable MRP: Cashiers cannot alter prices at the counter. All prices come from verified database records.
  • Audit Trail: Every stock movement is logged in stock_audit with permanent reasons (SALE, PURCHASE, BREAKAGE, SALES_RETURN).
  • Zero-Deletion Policy: Bills and invoices are never deleted. Errors are voided or reversed with mandatory manager justifications and offsetting journal vouchers.
  • Zero JS Float Arithmetic: All financial math runs through decimal.js, preventing rounding discrepancies in taxes or cash drawers.

Phase 1: Day 0 — Initial Store Setup

STEP 1

Organization & Outlet Configuration

The Syndicate Owner creates the business syndicate, configures outlet branches, local excise license numbers, and defines the local calendar timezone (e.g., IST UTC+330).

STEP 2

Staff Role & Action Permissions Setup

Create user logins for Managers, Cashiers, Stewards, and Office Admins. Assign granular action overrides (e.g., granting cost.view only to managers, discount.unlimited for senior staff).

STEP 3

Master Catalog Mapping & Price Lists

Map your outlet products against the centralized Master Catalog. Set the branch MRP and Janata counter prices. Barcodes, case pack sizes (e.g. 12 bottles/case), and category tax rates are instantly linked.

STEP 4

Physical Opening Inventory Count

Enter the initial bottle stock on hand per brand. This establishes baseline shelf numbers and forms the opening numbers for tomorrow's first Counter Sheet.

Phase 2: The Daily Operating Rhythm (Everyday Store Routine)

09:00 AM

Morning Shift Kickoff & Register Float

Cashier opens the cash drawer register by inputting the opening cash float (e.g., ₹5,000 in change). The system creates a new open shift session and auto-generates today's draft counter sheet carrying yesterday's closing stock.

10:00 AM - 10:00 PM

Continuous High-Speed Counter Billing & Inward

• Barcode POS Checkout: Scan bottle barcode, choose payment (Cash, UPI, Card, Khata). System prints tax receipt and deducts stock in real time.
• Invoice Receiving: Upload excise ICDC PDF. System checks bottle costs against historic records, increments inventory, and updates counter inward.
• Returns / Exchanges: Cashier records sealed bottle returns. System restructures stock, calculates net sales, and returns customer funds.

10:30 PM

Cashier Shift Closing & Cash Count

Cashier terminates their shift session. They input physical cash denominations counted in drawer. The system compares expected cash against counted cash and records any surplus/shortage.

11:00 PM

Physical Counter Sheet Closing & Daily Posting

Store manager inspects physical bottles remaining on shelves and enters closing counts. System checks Sold vs POS Billed variance. Manager posts the sheet, locking in unbilled counter sales into the general ledger.

11:15 PM

Manager Daily Closing Sign-Off

Manager reconciles total day net sales across all registers and counter sheets. Finalizes the day, locking the business date to prevent unauthorized retroactive edits.

Automated Double-Entry Accounting Architecture

You do not need an accountant on the sales floor. Every operational transaction automatically posts a balanced double-entry journal voucher into your chart of accounts:

1. Cash POS Sale

Dr CASH (Total gross collected)
Cr SALES (Net taxable revenue)
Cr OUTPUT_GST (Back-calculated tax)

2. Customer Sales Return

Dr SALES (Reversed sales share)
Dr OUTPUT_GST (Reversed tax share)
Cr UPI / CASH / CARD (Refunded amount)

3. Customer Khata Credit Sale

Dr SUNDRY_DEBTORS (Customer ledger)
Cr SALES (Net revenue)
Cr OUTPUT_GST (Tax portion)

4. Supplier Inward Invoice

Dr PURCHASES (Full invoice cost)
Cr SUNDRY_CREDITORS (Supplier ledger)
*No input tax credit as liquor has 0% ITC.
